#### Selected Projects

- Visual Token Efficiency in Large Vision-Language Models. Built a taxonomy of the field along two axes — three dimensions of efficiency and five paradigms: selection-based, merging-based, information-theoretic, and query-guided methods. Reproduced the LLaVA-Med inference pipeline and experimented with training-free token selection on medical VQA.

- Multimodal Medical Image Segmentation and Skin Disease Detection. Developed high-precision segmentation models (CycleGAN, Mamba-UNet) for cross-modal feature alignment across CT and MRI; built a YOLOv8-based skin-disease detection system with real-time video support, a self-collected dataset, and an interface with dynamic IoU / confidence control.

- GPT and Large Language Model Implementation from Scratch. Implemented a complete GPT forward pass in PyTorch: token and positional embedding, multi-head attention with causal masking, pre-norm residual blocks, training loop, and autoregressive generation.
